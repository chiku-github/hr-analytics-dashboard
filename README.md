## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive **Tableau visualization project** designed to analyze employee data and uncover key workforce insights.  
It helps HR teams and management understand **employee attrition, age distribution, job satisfaction, education background, and gender-wise trends**.

This dashboard supports **data-driven HR decision-making** by presenting complex HR metrics in a clear and actionable format.

---

## 🎯 Objective

- Analyze overall employee count and attrition rate  
- Identify departments with high attrition  
- Understand employee distribution by age group  
- Evaluate job satisfaction across job roles  
- Study attrition patterns by gender and education  

---

## 📝 Problem Statement

Organizations often struggle to:

- Track employee attrition trends  
- Identify departments at risk of high turnover  
- Understand workforce demographics  
- Measure job satisfaction across roles  

This HR Analytics dashboard solves these challenges using **interactive Tableau visualizations**.

## 📊 Key KPIs

- **Total Employees:** 882  
- **Active Employees:** 732  
- **Attrition Count:** 150  
- **Attrition Rate:** 17.01%  
- **Average Age:** 36.7 years  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🔹 Department-wise Attrition
- Sales department shows the **highest attrition**
- R&D and HR have comparatively lower attrition  

### 🔹 Age Group Analysis
- Highest employee concentration between **25–35 years**
- Attrition reduces as age increases  

### 🔹 Education Field-wise Attrition
- Life Sciences and Medical backgrounds show higher attrition
- Technical degrees have moderate attrition  

### 🔹 Gender-wise Attrition
- Male attrition is slightly higher compared to females
- Gender impact varies across different age groups  

### 🔹 Job Satisfaction
- Job satisfaction varies significantly across roles
- Sales and Laboratory roles show mixed satisfaction levels

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- 📊 **Bar Charts** – Age group & education analysis
